Privacy Policy

App: SpendSnap - Receipt Scanner (package: com.spendsnap.app)
Developer: Krakaa AI
Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the "SpendSnap - Receipt Scanner" mobile application (the "App"), published on Google Play by Krakaa AI ("we", "us", "the developer"). It explains what information the App collects, how it is used, who it is shared with, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: your email address, collected when you sign up with email and password or sign in with Google.
  • Receipt images: photos you take with your camera or select with the Android photo picker. The App does not access your full photo library; it only receives the images you choose.
  • Receipt details: information extracted from your receipts, such as merchant name, amount, date and category.
  • Purchase information: subscription and purchase status, processed through Google Play and RevenueCat. We do not receive or store your payment card details.
  • Advertising and device data: for free-tier users, Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) may collect advertising identifiers and basic device and usage data to serve ads.
  • Notifications: if you allow notifications, a device token is used to deliver them.

If you enable biometric unlock, authentication is handled by your device's operating system. We do not receive or store your fingerprint or face data.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To read receipts and display your receipts and spending reports.
  • To sync your data across your devices.
  • To provide and manage premium subscriptions.
  • To show advertising to free-tier users.
  • To send reminders and notifications you have enabled.
  • To maintain, secure and improve the App.

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following service providers. Each processes data under its own privacy policy.

  • Supabase: database, authentication and server functions.
  • Google Gemini API: receipt images are sent through our server for text extraction.
  • RevenueCat: in-app purchase and subscription processing.
  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob): advertising for free-tier users.
  • Google Sign-In: optional sign-in method.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Saving to Your Gallery

The App can save shareable report cards to your device gallery when you choose to. This only writes new images you create; the App does not read your existing gallery.

We keep your data while your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by emailing srujalbangare@gmail.com from the email address linked to your account. We will delete your data within 30 days of verifying your request.

6. Data Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections and stored with reputable providers. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us at the email below.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with data, contact us and we will delete it.
